Standard 60-Card Deck Guide

A rough starting point for a mono/two-color Standard-constructed deck. Adjust to taste.

Category Typical Count Notes
Lands 22–24 Roughly 40% of the deck (24 is a common default). Split by color pip weight, plus any utility lands.
Creatures 14–17 The core of most decks' game plan and win conditions.
Removal / Interaction 6–10 Instants/sorceries that answer opposing threats (kill spells, bounce, counters).
Other Spells 4–8 Card draw, pump/combat tricks, planeswalkers, artifacts, enchantments.
Nonland Subtotal ~36 The remaining 60% of the deck, split across the categories above.
Total 60 Minimum legal deck size — most decks stay at exactly 60.

Mana curve: aim for most of your nonland spells to cost 1–4 mana, tapering off sharply above 5, so you reliably have plays on curve.
